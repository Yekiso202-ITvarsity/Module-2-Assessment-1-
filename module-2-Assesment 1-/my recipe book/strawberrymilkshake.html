Strawberry Milkshake

Prep time: 15 mins

Yield: 4

Ingredients






Steps

  1. Rinse, hull, and halve the strawberries.
  2. Add strawberries to the blender first (helps smooth blending).
  3. Add ice cream on top, then pour in milk.
  4. Add sugar/honey and vanilla extract if desired.
  5. Pulse once, then blend on high for 20-30 seconds until creamy.
  6. Adjust thickness with more milk or sweetness with more sugar.
  7. Pour into a chilled glass and top with whipped cream and a strawberry slice.
