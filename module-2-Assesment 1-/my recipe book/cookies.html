Prep time: 60 mins

Yield: 4

Ingredients












Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C. Line tray with baking paper.
  2. Beat butter + both sugars until fluffy [2 min].
  3. Add egg and vanila, beat again.
  4. In another bowl mix flour, baking soda, cinnamon, salt, and oats.
  5. Add dry to wet, mix just until combimed. Don't overmix.
  6. Fold in pecans and raisins. If your raisins are hard, soak them in water for 5 minutes then drain- makes them juicy.
  7. Scoop 1 tablespoon balls, space them apart. They will spread.
  8. Bake 10-12 mins. They should look slightly underdone in the middle-they firm up as they cool.
  9. Cool on tray 5 mins, then on rack.
